09 — FAQ

Good To Know

What equipment is included?
Every session includes professional microphones, multi-camera 8K video, studio lighting and a sound-treated room. Specific gear scales with your package — just let us know what you're shooting and we'll set it up.
Can I bring guests?
Absolutely. The studio is set up for multi-guest podcasts and interviews, with comfortable seating and a lounge area. Let us know your headcount when you book so we can configure the room.
Do you offer editing?
Yes — editing is available as an add-on, from podcast cuts to short-form social clips. You can also take the raw files and edit them yourself; we deliver everything quickly either way.
Can I record music and podcast content in the same session?
You can. The room handles both, so it's easy to track vocals and record a podcast or interview in one block. Mention it when booking so we allow enough time and set up both configurations.
Is an engineer included?
On-site production support is available to help you get set up and capture clean audio and video. Full engineering or dedicated operator time can be added depending on your package.
How do bookings work?
Send an inquiry with your session type and preferred date, we'll confirm availability, and then you lock in your time. Simple — choose, pick a date, confirm, create.
Can I film reels and TikToks?
Definitely. The Content Creator package is built for exactly that — vertical-ready setups so you can batch reels, shorts and TikToks in a single session.
What file formats are delivered?
You'll receive standard high-quality video and audio files (such as MP4 and WAV), with raw or edited delivery depending on your package. Need a specific format or codec? Just ask.
