06 — FAQ

Good To Know

Is there a minimum order?
We work with teams of all sizes, from a handful of brokers to a full office. Tell us your team size in the quote form and we'll recommend the best approach and pricing for your run.
How fast is turnaround?
Timelines depend on garment type, quantity and decoration, but we pride ourselves on fast, dependable turnaround. We'll give you a clear delivery date up front — and stick to it.
What if I don't have a print-ready logo?
No problem. Send us whatever you have and our team will help prepare your artwork so it looks sharp on every garment. Artwork assistance is included.
Can we reorder for new hires later?
Absolutely — and that's a big part of why brokerages stay with us. We keep your branding and specs on file so reorders are consistent and effortless, whether it's one new hire or a whole new run.
Do you deliver?
Yes. Your finished order is delivered ready to wear. We're Calgary-based and coordinate delivery so your gear arrives where and when you need it.
