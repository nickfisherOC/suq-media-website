03 — The Process

Six Phases, One Accountable Team

A structured production path designed to remove risk and protect quality at every handoff.

Phase 01

Strategic Alignment

We map your vision, audience, budget and timeline into a clear production brief — defining product, fabric, quantities and targets before a single sample is made.

Phase 02

Design Engineering

Tech packs, garment specs, graded measurements and construction detailing translate your concept into a manufacturable blueprint.

Phase 03

Prototype Development

Factory-direct sampling brings the garment to life — fabric, fit, color and finish refined through controlled iterations.

Phase 04

Approval & Validation

Pre-production samples are reviewed and signed off against spec, so the bulk run is locked to an approved standard.

Phase 05

Scaled Manufacturing

Managed production with milestone tracking, controlled dyeing and in-line monitoring across the full run.

Phase 06

Logistics & Fulfillment

Freight, customs, Calgary QA, finishing and delivery — coordinated into one clean handoff to you or your customers.

06 — Logistics & Delivery

Freight, Customs & Delivery — Coordinated

Production is only half the job. We manage the movement of your goods across borders with the same oversight we bring to manufacturing — so timelines hold and nothing gets lost in transit.

Air Freight

Fastest

Indicative transit · 5–10 days

Speed-critical drops, launches and restocks. Higher cost, minimal wait — ideal when timing is everything.

Sea Freight

Most Economical

Indicative transit · 25–40 days

Cost-efficient at volume for planned production cycles. Best value when timelines are scheduled ahead.

Customs Coordination

Documentation, duties and clearance handled so shipments move without surprises.

Freight Management

Carrier selection, booking and routing optimized for your cost and timeline.

Delivery Oversight

Tracked, monitored and accountable from factory floor to final destination.
