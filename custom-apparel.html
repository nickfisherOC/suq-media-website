03 — The Process

Your Order, Start to Finish

A clear process designed to remove risk and keep quality consistent at every step.

Step 01

Your Quote & Brief

Tell us what you need — products, quantities, timing and budget — and we'll scope the order with you.

Step 02

Artwork & Proof

Send your logo and our team prepares artwork and a proof, so you approve exactly how it will look before production.

Step 03

Garments Sourced

We source the right garments and blanks for your order through trusted suppliers.

Step 04

Sample / Approval

Where needed, we confirm colours, sizing and placement so the run is locked to an approved standard.

Step 05

Printing & Embroidery in Calgary

Your order is printed and embroidered in-house at our Calgary shop, then quality-checked.

Step 06

Pickup or Shipping

Pick up at our Calgary location, or we ship to Edmonton, across Alberta, and throughout Canada and the U.S.

04 — Decoration Options

Printed & Embroidered In-House

Every order is decorated at our Calgary shop. We'll recommend the right method based on the garment, artwork, intended use and budget.

  • Screen Printing

    Bold, durable prints — ideal for logos and larger runs of tees, hoodies and workwear.

  • Embroidery

    A premium, professional finish for polos, caps, jackets and uniforms.

  • DTF Printing

    Full-colour, detailed transfers for complex artwork across a range of garments.

  • Names, Numbers & Personalization

    Add staff names, numbers or departments for uniforms and crews.

  • Artwork & Proofing

    Send your logo and we'll prepare print-ready artwork and a proof for your approval.

Not Sure Which Method?

Send us your logo and tell us how the apparel will be used. Our team will recommend the right decoration method for your garment and budget, then prepare a proof so you know exactly what you're ordering.

05 — Quality Control & Finishing

Nothing Ships Until It's Right

Every order is printed, embroidered, finished and inspected at our Calgary shop before customer pickup or shipping — so you know it's done right.

QA

Final Inspection

Garments checked against approved samples for measurement, construction, color and defects.

FINISH

Embellishment

Final decoration applied and verified — nothing leaves without meeting spec.

PRINT

Screen and DTF printing executed and quality-checked for placement, opacity and durability.

STITCH

PACK

Packaging

SHIP

Fulfillment

Verified, packed and dispatched — to you, your warehouse, or directly to customers.
