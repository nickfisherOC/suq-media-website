Meet The Hosts

Kerry

Co-Founder & Host

The Suq Media Podcast

Kerry's journey through addiction, healing, self-discovery and personal transformation has shaped the heart behind Suq Media. With 23 years clean and sober, 13 years of postsecondary education and a lifetime of real-world experiences, she brings raw honesty, emotional depth and a passion for understanding the human condition.

Through storytelling, wellness, and meaningful conversations, Kerry hopes to inspire growth, emotional maturity, and hope in others while challenging mainstream narratives around healing and personal evolution.

Krzysztof

Co-Founder & Host

The Suq Media Podcast

As co-founder of Suq Media, Krzysztof leads the company's audio, video and digital production with a unique blend of technical skill and creative vision. With a background in IT, telecommunications and computer science, he now uses media as a platform for transformation, recovery and social impact.

Having overcome addiction himself, Kris is passionate about creating authentic content that breaks stigma, encourages healing and gives a voice to stories that matter. Outside the studio, he finds inspiration in woodworking and aviation.

Become A Guest

Have A Story Worth Sharing?

We're looking for guests with real stories, lived experience, and genuine insight. If you've been through something, learned something, or built something worth talking about — we want to hear from you.

  • You have a lived experience in health, wellness, recovery, or personal transformation
  • You're an expert, practitioner, or thought leader in a relevant field
  • You have a unique perspective on human potential, relationships, or community
  • You're comfortable being honest, vulnerable, and real on camera
  • You align with Suq Media's values of healing, hope and purpose
