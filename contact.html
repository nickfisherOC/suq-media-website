05 — FAQ

Questions, Answered

How do I book the studio?
Head to the Studio Rental page, pick a session, and send a booking request with your preferred date. We'll confirm availability and lock in your time.
Can I appear on a podcast?
Yes — we're always looking for guests with real stories and lived experience. Apply through the Become a Guest form and our team will review it.
Do you work with brands?
Absolutely. From content and campaigns to sponsorships and custom apparel, we partner with brands of all sizes. Choose "Business Partnership" in the form above and tell us what you have in mind.
Can I submit music?
If you're an independent artist with something real to say, we'd love to hear it. Submit through the Music page and we'll be in touch if it's a fit.
Do you offer production services?
Yes — podcast and music production, multi-camera video, content creation and full creative production. Tell us about your project in the contact form and we'll map out the right approach.
How quickly do you respond?
We typically respond to serious inquiries within 1–2 business days. Every message is reviewed by the SUQ Media team.
