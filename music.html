River James

"I write songs because some things can't be said in ordinary language."

River James is an independent folk artist whose music blends raw storytelling with cinematic instrumentation. Drawing from personal experiences of loss, healing and hope, his songs have quietly amassed a dedicated following of listeners who feel deeply understood.
